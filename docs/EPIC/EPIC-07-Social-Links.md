# EPIC-07 — Social Links

## Description
Provides links and visibility to external professional profiles and social networks (e.g., GitHub, LinkedIn, Twitter/X).

---

### US-022 — View Social Profiles
**User Story:**
> As a visitor,  
> I want to see the Portfolio Owner's social and professional profiles,  
> so that I can connect with them on external platforms.

**Priority:** `Must Have`

---

### US-023 — Manage Social Links
**User Story:**
> As the Portfolio Owner,  
> I want to manage my social links,  
> so that visitors always have access to my current profiles.

**Priority:** `Must Have`
